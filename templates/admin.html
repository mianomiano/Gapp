Logo

Upload a logo image and it replaces the name in the top header, and also shows full-width on the About page. With no image, the header falls back to the logo text. Leave both empty and the app is unnamed.

The header scales any logo down to fit its 36px slot, so it can never push the buttons out — but a wide, short mark reads best. Transparent PNG is ideal.

Telegram / sharing link

Used by the Forward / Copy-link / Share buttons so they hand out your Telegram mini-app address (t.me/…) instead of the raw https tunnel URL.

Link becomes t.me/<bot>/<short name>. Leave the short name blank to use t.me/<bot>?startapp.

Contact — where inquiries go

When someone sends a Job / Advertising message from the About page, the bot forwards it (with their @username) to this Telegram chat. A copy is always kept in the Messages tab even if this is blank.

Tip: message @userinfobot on Telegram to get your numeric chat id. You must have started your bot once so it can DM you.
